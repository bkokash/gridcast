GridCast privacy

Draft, 25 November 2026. Not legal advice; review before publishing widely.

What stays on your PC

Your layouts, presets, sites, logins and settings are stored only on your PC (in %APPDATA%GridCast). We don't receive them. The websites you open in GridCast see you as they would in any browser.

Casting

The TV picture goes directly from your PC to your TV over your own network. To start casting, the TV loads GridCast's small receiver page from GitHub Pages.

Licence check

When you activate a licence key, and about once a month after that, GridCast sends the key, an activation id and your PC's name to Polar (polar.sh), which sells GridCast licences for us. Polar also handles your payment details and invoices under its own privacy policy. We see your order and email address there.

Updates

GridCast asks GitHub whether a new version exists, at start and every 6 hours. GitHub sees your IP address, as for any download.
