Questions

What do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and a TV with Chromecast or Google TV, wireless display (Miracast) or an HDMI cable. The PC and TV must be on the same network for casting.

Does it work with Samsung TVs?

Yes, through wireless display: in GridCast choose Cast → Wireless display, pick the TV, and the grid moves there. A VPN on the PC can block wireless displays.

Is there a subscription?

No. Personal and Business are one-time purchases and include all updates to version 1.x.

Can I move my licence to another PC?

Yes: in GridCast, Settings → Licence → Remove from this PC, then enter the key on the new PC.

Does it need the internet?

Only for the websites you show, for activating your licence (then about once a month), and for updates. Casting goes straight from your PC to the TV on your network.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

For now, yes: GridCast isn't code-signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
